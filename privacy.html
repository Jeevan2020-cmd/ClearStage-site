How I look after your information

Plain English, no small print. Last updated 22 November 2026.

Questions or requests about your data: email clearstageinfo@gmail.com.

Services that help me run this

Like most small businesses, I use trusted providers to run the site, store information and take payments. They only use your data to provide their service to me:

  • GitHub hosts this website.
  • FormSubmit passes your assessment answers to my inbox.
  • Google (Gmail) is where I receive and send email.
  • Microsoft (OneDrive) stores my list of assessment results.
  • Calendly handles call bookings.
  • Tally runs the waitlist and email sign-up form.
  • Payhip, with Stripe or PayPal, sells and delivers the guide and processes payments.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts on this site, so your browser connects to Google when a page loads.
  • Anthropic (Claude), an AI assistant, helps me organise new assessment results into my list and draft the results emails I send. I read every email before it goes out.

Some of these providers are based in, or store data in, the United States. Where that happens, the transfer is covered by safeguards recognised under UK law, such as the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

How long I keep it

  • Assessment results and enquiries: up to 2 years after we were last in touch, then deleted.
  • Purchase records: 6 years, because UK tax rules require it.
  • Email list: until you unsubscribe.

Cookies

This site doesn't set its own cookies or use tracking or advertising tools. The booking, sign-up and checkout services above may set cookies on their own pages when you use them; their privacy policies explain how.

Your rights

You can ask me to:

  • give you a copy of the information I hold about you
  • correct anything that's wrong
  • delete your information
  • stop or limit how I use it, including stopping all marketing emails
  • give you your information in a format you can take elsewhere

Just email clearstageinfo@gmail.com. I'll reply within one month. It's free.

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your data, please tell me first so I can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes

If I change how I use your information, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
